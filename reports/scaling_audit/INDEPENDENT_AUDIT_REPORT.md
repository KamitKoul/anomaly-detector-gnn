## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Mandate

An independent scientific audit was commissioned to determine whether the results presented in the *Consolidated Scientific Monograph: Background-Data Scaling Study for the LHC Olympics PointNet–GAT Anomaly Detector* were genuinely produced by independent training runs across the 1,000,000-event LHC Olympics dataset, or whether implementation errors, data substitutions, or reporting defects caused different experiment tiers to appear identical.

### Core Audit Verdict: **CLASSIFICATION C — SCIENTIFICALLY BLOCKED**
The reported scaling study results **cannot be accepted as scientifically valid empirical measurements** of the LHC Olympics dataset. 

While the software architecture, dataset split generators, and test suites are mathematically sound and well-engineered, the reported experimental metrics (including validation losses, matched ROC-AUCs of $0.4856$, and mass-sculpting $1/\text{JSD}$ values) were **not produced by training on LHC collision data**. They were generated by a synthetic mock smoke-test harness running on 600 uniform random numbers on macOS, which was subsequently multiplied and presented across all 7 dataset tiers in the comparison tables.

## 8. AUDIT 7 & 8 — Statistical Reporting & Checkpoint Audits

1. **Uncertainty Conflation:** The $\pm$ value reported as $0.4856 \pm 0.0349$ is the sample standard deviation of 500 bootstrap replicas on a single 500-event mock evaluation. It does not measure model variance across independent training seeds.

3. **Power-Law Fit Degeneracy:** Fitting $M(N) = M_\infty - a N^{-\alpha}$ to data where all points are identical yields a trivial flat line with zero slope ($\alpha = 0$), not a physical scaling law.
